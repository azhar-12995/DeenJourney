Account and data deletion

Last updated: 7 October 2026 · com.deenjourney.app

You can request deletion of your Deen Journey account and associated data without downloading the app or signing into it.

Send a deletion request

  1. Email deenjourney12995@gmail.com, preferably from the email address linked to your Deen Journey account.
  2. Use the subject Deen Journey account deletion and say whether you want the whole account and associated data deleted, or only specified records. Include correction reports/support messages if you want those removed too.
  3. We will verify account ownership, process the request and confirm the outcome by email. If you cannot access your account email, explain that in your request so we can discuss verification.

Compose deletion request

If the button does not open an email app, copy the address above and send your request from your usual email service. Opening the email composer does not submit a request; you must send the email.

Do not send your password or parent PIN. A deletion request does not require you to share either.

Data covered

Account deletion covers the Firebase authentication account and its synced family profiles, bookmarks/saved items, notes, highlights, learning/memorization progress, counters, daily activity and Zakat history. Correction reports and support records can be included in the email request. Any required legal retention and its period will be explained when the request is processed. Backup copies may remain until their provider's normal backup expiry.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data but does not by itself remove your cloud account. Previously exported or shared files and recipient-held copies are outside our control.

Delete in the app

Open Privacy & data → Delete account and confirm after entering your password when requested. The current in-app flow does not automatically delete correction reports, so use the email path for those records or if the in-app flow fails.
