2. Cloud sync and device backups

Cloud sync is enabled by default in the current app. You can switch it off under Privacy & data → Cloud sync. Disabling sync stops future app sync requests; it does not delete information already stored in your account. Some features still require internet access, including login, correction submissions, downloads and online content.

Android backup and device-transfer services may back up or restore local settings and user records according to your device settings. The app excludes downloaded audio from its backup configuration. Operating-system backup is separate from the app's Cloud sync switch.

3. Online services and sharing

We use Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for account access, synced records and correction reports. Online Quran/tafsir/word resources use Quran.com/Quran Foundation; recitation audio uses EveryAyah and Islamic Network; hadith downloads use the hadith-api project through jsDelivr. Metal-price and currency requests use Gold API and ExchangeRate-API. These services receive the resources or requests needed to provide the selected feature, as well as normal network metadata. We do not send them your app password, personal notes or Zakat input amounts as part of content/price requests.

When you choose Share or Export, the text or file is passed to the app or recipient you select. Those services handle it under their own policies. External links open websites with their own data practices.

The current app has no advertising SDK, Firebase Analytics SDK or Crashlytics SDK integrated. Settings labels alone do not enable such collection. We do not sell your personal data or use it for targeted advertising. Service-provider logging is separate from app analytics.

Provider information: Firebase privacy and security and Google Privacy Policy. This policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages; hosting requests are subject to GitHub's Privacy Statement. This website adds no analytics scripts or advertising cookies.

4. Permissions

  • Location: optional GPS detection for prayer times and Qibla; manual city selection is available.
  • Notifications and alarm access: used for prayer/learning reminders and requested through Android settings or permission prompts where required.
  • Internet and foreground audio: used for account services, online resources, downloads and background recitation/adhan playback.

You can revoke permissions in your device settings. The current Android app does not request access to your contacts, camera or microphone, and does not record a child's voice.

5. Family and children's information

Child profiles are managed under a parent or guardian's account; children do not need a separate login for a family profile. A parent can choose a nickname, avatar and age group and manage the profile's learning records. We do not request a separate child's email or exact date of birth for these profiles. If cloud sync is enabled, profile and progress records are sent to the parent's Firebase account. Parents can request access or deletion using the contact details below.

Parents should supervise use and sharing. A child profile is not a guarantee that every search, external link or sharing route is blocked in the current release. Do not put unnecessary sensitive information into profile names, notes or reports.

6. Storage, security and retention

App records are stored in the app's local storage and, for synced records, in Firebase. Online services use HTTPS. Cloud providers may process information outside your country. We use authentication and the platform's app-storage protections, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Account and synced records are retained while you use your account, unless you remove them or request deletion. Local files remain until removed through available app/device controls or when the app's storage is cleared. Uninstalling the app does not delete your Firebase account or cloud records. Copies in operating-system or service-provider backups may remain until those services' normal backup expiry.

Correction/support records are handled separately from ordinary profile sync. The current in-app deletion flow does not automatically remove correction reports; email us to request their removal. If a specific record must be retained for a legal requirement, we will explain the reason and applicable retention period when handling your request.

7. Your choices and account deletion

You can change profile/settings information, manage saved items, turn off cloud sync, revoke permissions and export your local family records under Privacy & data → Export my data. For access, correction or other privacy requests, email deenjourney12995@gmail.com.

To delete your account in the app, open Privacy & data → Delete account, enter your password when requested and confirm. This flow is intended to remove your Firebase authentication account and synced family profiles, saved items, notes, highlights, progress, counters, daily records, Zakat history and backed-up account preferences, then clear the app's local user records. It depends on successful authentication and cloud deletion; if an error appears, contact us. To remove correction/support records as well, include them in your email request.

You can also request account and associated-data deletion without reinstalling or signing into the app using our account deletion request page. We verify ownership before acting and confirm the outcome by email. Never send your password or parent PIN. Exported/shared copies held by you or a recipient are outside the app's deletion control.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The updated date appears at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate app or service notice.
